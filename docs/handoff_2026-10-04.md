## What this session found

1. **Labels: train on the report labels (`teach4.csv`), not OOF pseudo-labels.** Same image recipe, three label
   sets: teach4 only (R5t) 0.922 > R2 mix (R3) 0.920 > heavy OOF (R4) 0.917 on the LB; gold-58 ranks them the
   same (0.905 > 0.898 > 0.891). OOF mixing fits the report labels better and the expert labels worse.
2. **Image recipe (fold-0 A/Bs, ConvNeXt-nano, 872 val studies; training noise ≈ 0.002):** 336 px +0.0025
   (borderline; helps MCL / lateral meniscus), 18–20 epochs +0.0045 — both adopted. Rejected or neutral:
   slot-aware windows, wider span (4–96 %), tissue-centred crop, 40 windows/study, ConvNeXt-tiny, ResNet-50,
   CoAtNet-1 (ρ 0.97 with nano), gold-58 label calibration (LOO 0.835 vs 0.896).
3. **Our own models do not stack with each other** (R5t + R3 + R4 average lower than R5t alone on gold-58); vs
   the public Raptor family they correlate 0.87, so a small blend weight is the right use.
4. Kaggle GPU quota is **30 h/week** (the quota API misreports 6 h); max 2 concurrent GPU sessions; 1 TPU session.
